## Human Review Items Carried Forward

1. **Databricks Unity AI Gateway** — straddles `catalog`, `context`, and `governance/gateway`. Recommend reviewing for addition as a reference implementation under a `gateway` product in the `governance` domain in a separate run.
2. **Google Cloud Knowledge Catalog `knowledge_catalog` capability** — currently placed under `catalog` product. Review whether a distinct sub-capability grouping is warranted as adoption matures.
3. **Snowflake Cortex Analyst** — added under `semantic` (knowledge) only. Recommend a separate agentic domain coverage review.
4. **Graph RAG in regulated financial services** — third-wave knowledge agent trend (Continuus AI, May 2026) is significant for KYC/compliance. Recommend a dedicated Graph RAG research run.
5. **Neo4j licensing** — flagged in notes for both `retrieval` and `knowledge` products. Enterprise architects evaluating Graph RAG stacks should review total cost of ownership.
6. **Permission-aware retrieval × governance/entitlement** — AWS Bedrock + Verified Permissions pattern (published 2026-06-22) has direct financial services applicability. Recommend cross-domain review with `governance` entitlement product.
